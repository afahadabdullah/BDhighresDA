assembled from noise.

A rectified-flow U-Net learns what 5 km daily rainfall over Bangladesh looks like. At analysis time its weights are frozen — rain gauges and satellite footprints steer the sampling trajectory instead, by pushing on the velocity field the network returns.

01

Three kinds of data, three different jobs

Most confusion about this system comes from collapsing these together. A dataset here is either what the network learned from, or what conditions it, or what the assimilation pushes against — never two at once. That separation is what makes the withheld-gauge scores mean anything.

Learn

The prior is trained on CHIRPS

Flow matching on 1981–2018 daily CHIRPS at 0.05°, 128×128 crops. This teaches the network the distribution of plausible fine-scale rainfall: intensity statistics, spatial texture, where structure sits relative to terrain.

CHIRPS 0.05° · 1981–2018
Condition

CPC and ERA5 set the situation

Seven dynamic channels plus terrain and season enter the network on every forward pass. The target is the standardised residual from CPC, so a zero residual reproduces the coarse analysis and the flow supplies the 5 km correction.

CPC 0.5° · ERA5 tcwv, cape, u10, v10, msl
Assimilate

BMD and IMERG enter only at sampling

Never a training target, never a conditioning channel. They appear once, inside a Gaussian likelihood, whose gradient bends the sampling trajectory. The network's weights never see them.

~35 BMD gauges · IMERG S04 (0.4°)

Why it matters: CPC conditioned the prior and is the residual baseline, so agreement with CPC is not verification. CHIRPS is the training target, so agreement with CHIRPS is training-family agreement. IMERG was assimilated, so agreement with IMERG is observation adherence. Only a BMD station that was held out of the likelihood is independent evidence.

03

What the network is actually asked to learn

Rectified flow builds a straight line in field space between a noise draw x₀ and a real rainfall field x₁, and asks the network for one thing: the constant velocity along that line, u = x₁ − x₀, given only the point xt you are standing on, the time t, and the conditioning. Drag t and watch the four panels — the same t drives the sampler in section 05.

forward path  x_t = t·x₁ + (1−t)·x₀
training loss  ‖ u_θ(x_t, t, c) − (x₁ − x₀) ‖²
denoised estimate  x̂₁ = x_t + (1−t)·u
noise estimate    x̂₀ = x_t − t·u
score                   ∇ log p_t(x_t) = −x̂₀ / (1−t)
and therefore  Δu = ((1−t)/t) · Δ(score)

Why this algebra is the whole trick

A velocity network and a score network are the same object in different clothes. Because the interpolant is linear and x₀ is Gaussian, you can convert between them exactly — no extra training, no second model.

So the score-based data assimilation of Rozet & Louppe and Manshausen et al. transfers directly: work out how much the observations want to change the score, multiply by (1−t)/t, and add it to the velocity. One trained network does downscaling and assimilation both.

Training draws t from a logit-normal density (t = sigmoid(z), z ~ N(0,1)), which concentrates samples near t = 0.5 where the velocity field is hardest to learn. Conditioning dropout is 0 in the production configuration, so this checkpoint has no unconditional branch to spare and classifier-free guidance stays at w = 1.

04

The network that returns the velocity

An ADM-style U-Net, deliberately self-contained so it runs on an air-gapped HPC node. Roughly 20–40 M parameters. Two things make it a conditional flow network rather than a plain segmentation U-Net: the time t enters every residual block as a FiLM scale-and-shift, and the conditioning stack is re-encoded and injected at every resolution instead of only being concatenated at the input.

Hover a level. Channel widths come from base_channels: 96 with channel_mult: [1, 2, 3, 4], two residual blocks per level, four attention heads, dropout 0.2.
Time conditioning

A Fourier embedding of the continuous t ∈ [0,1] goes through a two-layer MLP, then each residual block projects it to a per-channel (scale, shift) applied after the second GroupNorm. The network therefore knows how far along the path it is — which it must, because the right velocity depends on it.

Multiscale conditioning

A separate encoder strides the conditioning stack down to 128², 64², 32² and 16², and each feature map is added into the matching encoder level through a zero-initialised 1×1 projection. Zero-init means the network starts life identical to the unconditioned one and learns to lean on CPC rather than being forced to.

Land mask

CHIRPS is land-only. Masked cells are held at transform(0 mm) = −μ/σ, not at a literal 0.0 — pinning them to zero would pin the Bay of Bengal to a moderate rain rate, and the global attention blocks would happily carry it inland.

05

Assimilation, one sampling step at a time

Now run the path forward with observations attached. At every step the sampler asks the frozen network for a velocity, reconstructs the clean field x̂₁ from it, pushes x̂₁ through the observation operator, and differentiates the Gaussian log-likelihood back through the network to get a gradient on xt. That gradient, scaled by (1−t)/t, is added to the velocity. Turn the observation streams on and off and watch the increment appear.

Gauges — a soft likelihood

A gauge is a point; a grid cell is 5 km of area. The production likelihood adds a 0.10 measurement SD to a 0.25 representativeness SD, giving about 0.269 in transformed units — representativeness dominates. Each member gets its own perturbed draw from that error model, because assimilating one identical observation into every member is the classic route to an under-dispersive analysis.

A point gauge only touches four cells through the bilinear operator, so the gradient is a spike. Convolving it with a 6-cell Gaussian spreads one station over roughly 33 km — defensible against the ~146 km measured variogram range of the BMD network, but it is an assertion about the background covariance, and the code labels it as an approximation. Set the spread to 0 above to see the bullseyes it was introduced to fix.

IMERG — an areal likelihood

A satellite footprint really is, to first order, an area average, so the forward operator is exact: one 0.4° value is the mean of an 8×8 block of 0.05° cells. No smoothing is needed or wanted — the observation is already areal.

Retrieval errors are correlated across neighbouring footprints, so perturbations are drawn with a 0.75-footprint correlation length; white noise would average away and add no spread. No fitted bias map is applied in the frozen production arm, which is why IMERG agreement is reported as adherence to an assimilated product, not as skill.

Integrator

50 Heun steps on a uniform schedule, two bounded Langevin corrector steps per level. The correctors are there because guidance is mode-seeking: re-injecting entropy at fixed t stops integration error and the likelihood pull from compounding along a trajectory.

Prior temperature is the real spread knob, and production sets it to T = 1.0. Inflating the unguided background is a trap: tempering happens in transformed space, and Jensen's inequality then biases the mm-space ensemble mean high — once by +6.4 mm on a 1.7 mm day.

06

Thirty members, and what they are for

Each member starts from an independent noise draw and carries its own perturbed observations, so the spread measures two things: how ambiguous the downscaling is given CPC and ERA5, and how much the observation error model admits. Watch what happens near the gauges — the members agree where they were pulled and disagree where nothing constrained them. That behaviour is the argument for inflating the prior rather than the analysis.

Push the gauge weight to w = 4: the assimilated-gauge error keeps falling toward the observation-noise floor while the withheld-gauge error stops improving. Buying a closer fit at the stations you assimilated is not the same as buying skill. Turn IMERG off and the withheld number degrades where no gauge is nearby. In the real system the primary metric is CRPS over five spatially rotated folds, so every eligible station is withheld exactly once.

07

Reading the product honestly

A generative model will hand you a beautifully textured 5 km field whether or not that texture is in the right place. The evidence hierarchy exists so that a sharp-looking map is never mistaken for a demonstrated one.

The date contract

For a public BMD label day D, the gauges and the prepared IMERG cover the BMD window ending 03 UTC on D, while the CPC, ERA5 and CHIRPS background record is D − 1. A calendar-day product cannot quietly stand in for the BMD-aligned file, and every map title, station table and daily comparison has to carry the same convention.

What 0.05° does not buy you

An output grid at 0.05° does not by itself establish 0.05° information. The claim needs the exact decomposition into S04 footprint means and below-footprint residuals, spectra and variograms, increment-locality diagnostics — and above all, positive sub-footprint placement skill at withheld gauges. Texture alone is not enough, and neither is agreement with CHIRPS.

The other design point. A companion page builds the same posterior on a graph instead of a grid: a diffusion prior whose backbone is a graph network on a global icosahedral mesh, where observations are nodes rather than interpolation targets and localisation comes from the receptive field rather than a spread length. It carries a side-by-side comparison of the two — including the one axis where the rectified-flow path used here is measurably better: with V(t) = R + Γ(1−t)²/t² the observations carry more than half weight over roughly 36 of the 50 steps, against about 7 of 20 for an EDM diffusion schedule running from σ = 80.
